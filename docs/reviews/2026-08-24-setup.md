## Milestone boundary

unit/property/corpus tests, maintained root runners, fuzzing, benchmarks, CI,
release workflow, and Alire index publication are intentionally not claimed by
this commit. They remain required before final delivery, and implementation is
still gated on Serde and Type IR consumer review of the published draft.
